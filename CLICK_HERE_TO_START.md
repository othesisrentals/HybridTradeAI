## ?? STILL STUCK?

### Can't find the backtick key?
Look for the key that has **~** and **`** on it. It's in the top-left area of your keyboard, usually right next to the number **1** key.

### Terminal won't open?
Try these alternatives:
1. Click "**View**" at the very top of Cursor
2. Click "**Terminal**"
3. Click "**New Terminal**"

### Nothing happens after typing the command?
Make sure you:
1. Pressed **Enter** after pasting the command
2. Waited at least 30 seconds
3. Are connected to the internet
